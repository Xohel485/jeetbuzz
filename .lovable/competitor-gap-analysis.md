## Where they beat us
1. **Backlink volume/authority** — 846–3,633 ref domains vs our 16. Biggest single gap.
   Note: much of theirs is spam/PBN, but they also hold real assets (jeetbuzzhelp.com,
   sportslivehub.com feeding thousands of links).
2. **Transactional URL structure** — jeetbuzz.cc ranks #1 with `/bd/bn/login` and
   `/bd/bn/forgot-password`; 64% of its traffic comes from just those two URLs.
   They own the *action* intent, not the guide intent.
3. **Brand = operator domain** — they are the operator; Google treats them as the entity.
   We are a guide site and must win on informational + problem-solving intent instead.
4. **Keyword footprint** — 300–750 ranking keywords each vs effectively 0 indexed for us.
5. **App-download intent** — `/bd/bn/app-download` ranks top-5; our /download cluster
   still splits authority across 3 URLs.

## Where we can beat them
- Their content is thin operator UI pages, no editorial depth, no E-E-A-T, no author.
- Their anchor profiles are toxic; a clean profile ages better.
- Long-tail Bengali problem queries (withdrawal pending, OTP not received, deposit failed,
  KYC, promo code terms) are barely covered by any of them.

## Fixing plan

- Verify every money page is indexed in GSC; submit sitemap children individually.
- Disavow the Fiverr/PBN referring domains (16 links, 14 of them junk) so the first real
  links are not diluted.
- Keep only self-referencing canonicals on /download, /jeetbuzz-apk-download,
  /jeetbuzz-download-for-android -> consolidate to one canonical.

### P1 — Own the action intent in Bengali (weeks 2–4)
- Strengthen /login, /password-reset, /jeetbuzz-otp-not-received, /download with
  Bengali-first H1 + step screenshots + FAQ, matching jeetbuzz.cc's ranking URL intent.
- Add a Bengali "forgot password" dedicated section/page mirroring their #2 traffic URL.

### P2 — Link acquisition, clean (weeks 3–8)

- Digital PR angle: "how to verify a licensed operator" data pieces.

### P3 — Topical expansion (ongoing)
- Publish long-tail Bengali problem posts at 2/week; each links to a hub within 1 hop.
- Track position for the 20 GSC queries already recorded in batch reports.
